## The situation

The producer's energy reporting was built once a year from utility invoices and production totals — accurate enough for a compliance filing, but useless for finding where energy was actually being lost on a specific kiln line, or for tracking whether an efficiency measure had actually worked after it was implemented.

## What we did

1. Ran a combustion analysis and thermal imaging survey across the kiln line to establish where the thermal specific energy consumption baseline actually stood, rather than relying on the design-spec figure.
2. Installed 14 metering points across the electrical distribution and combustion air systems feeding the line, sized to the loads actually present rather than a generic template.
3. Connected the meters into a live monitoring feed, benchmarked against the specific energy consumption baseline set during the initial audit, so deviations show up in days rather than at the next annual review.
4. Documented the estimation method and flagged uncertainty on any submetering gaps, rather than presenting a false precision the data didn't support.

## The result

The plant now tracks kiln-line energy performance continuously against its own baseline instead of an annual industry estimate, catching drift from combustion tuning and process changes as it happens.

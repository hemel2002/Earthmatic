## The situation

The group's three manufacturing sites each tracked energy and fuel data independently, in different formats, with no shared consolidation approach. Ahead of an SBTi target submission, the group needed one defensible Scope 1 and 2 number — not three site reports stitched together after the fact.

## What we did

1. Defined the organisational and operational boundary across the three sites, documenting the consolidation approach (operational control) so it would hold up under third-party review.
2. Issued a standardised data request to each site covering fuel purchases, refrigerant top-ups, backup generator run-hours and purchased electricity, with a gap-filling protocol for incomplete records.
3. Applied country-specific grid emission factors with sources version-controlled, rather than a single blended regional average.
4. Delivered a base-year inventory report structured for third-party verification, plus a recalculation policy covering future site additions and process changes.
5. Handed over the full calculation model — not a locked PDF — so the group's own EHS team could update it between engagements.

## The result

The group entered SBTi target validation with a single, source-documented Scope 1 and 2 baseline across all three sites, with every emission factor traceable to its origin.
